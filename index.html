What We Do

Furniture • Architectural Timber • Custom Manufacturing

At Beck Custom Timber, we bridge the gap between architectural vision and raw natural beauty. We selectively source premium timber components to craft functional, durable pieces tailored exactly to architectural and residential specifications.

Our Capabilities

MachiningPrecision squaring, dressing, and flattening rough slabs to required structural thicknesses.
ResawingSplitting large raw timbers down into custom dimensions with minimal waste.
ProfilingCreating custom structural molding, cladding shapes, and exquisite edge details.
Joinery & ReproductionTraditional mortise-and-tenon craftsmanship and historical timber element reproduction.
